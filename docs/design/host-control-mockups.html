WinDock: host control page and QR pairing

The computer-side page shown in the macOS menu-bar popover, the Windows tray flyout, a detached window and a plain browser, plus the phone screens added for QR pairing. Spec: docs/design/HOST-CONTROL-PAGE.md. Native menus, the camera feed and the desktop around the popover are drawn approximately.

The QR codes are real and scannable: they encode the sample payloads in spec section 9 (version 8, error correction M). Screenshot one frame: open this file with #solo=<id> at the frame’s size.
